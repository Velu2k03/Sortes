# Sortes Tarot App - Design System

## Colors
- **Background**: Deep Navy (`#0a0a1a`) - Primary background for a mystical, infinite night sky feel.
- **Accents**: Gold (`#d4af37` or `#e8c353`) - For interactive elements, celestial motifs, and highlights.
- **Text (Primary)**: Off-White (`#e8e4d9`) - Soft on the eyes against the dark background.
- **Text (Muted)**: Muted Indigo/Gray (`#8a8a9d`) - For secondary text and captions.
- **Surface**: Dark Indigo (`#15152a`) - For cards, sheets, and elevated elements.
- **Borders**: Subtle Gold/Indigo (`#2a2a40` or rgba gold).

## Typography
- **Headings**: `Cormorant Garamond` (serif) - Elegant, spiritual, traditional.
- **Body**: `Inter` (sans-serif) - Clean, legible for card meanings and UI elements.

## Spacing & Sizing
- Minimum tap target: `44px` for mobile.
- Generous whitespace around readings to reduce cognitive load.

## Motion & Interaction
- **Card Flip**: 600ms ease-in-out 3D flip.
- **Fade Ins**: 300ms ease for page transitions.
- **Reduced Motion**: Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for all animations.
- **Interactions**: Tap to flip, swipe to dismiss sheets. Mimics physical ritual.
